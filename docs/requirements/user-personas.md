# User Personas
## Indian Government Scheme & Scholarship Assistant

This document defines realistic, non-stereotypical personas representing core citizen segments across India.

---

### Persona 1: Rohan Sharma (Student Aspirant)

- **Age**: 20
- **Gender**: Male
- **Location**: Tier-2 City (Bhopal, Madhya Pradesh) - Urban
- **Category**: OBC (Non-Creamy Layer)
- **Occupation / Status**: 2nd Year B.Tech Computer Science Student
- **Annual Family Income**: ₹2,20,000 / year (Father is a private shop assistant)
- **Special Status**: First-generation college student

#### Goals
- Find merit-cum-means scholarships to cover annual tuition and hostel fees.
- Discover government laptop/device assistance schemes for technical education.

#### Pain Points
- Confused by conflicting eligibility criteria across Central sector scholarships and MP state portals.
- Misses application deadlines due to lack of timely notifications.

#### Information Known vs. Unknown
- **Can Provide**: Academic marks (10th/12th/CGPA), income certificate number, caste category, institute AISHE code.
- **May Not Know**: Specific scheme codes, difference between National Scholarship Portal (NSP) schemes and state-run portal schemes.

#### Typical Questions
- *"Which scholarships can I apply for with 82% in 12th and ₹2.2L income?"*
- *"Can I avail both a Central scholarship and a State fee reimbursement?"*

#### Expected System Interaction
Quick profile entry via wizard → Instant list of eligible scholarships ranked by deadline → Clear document checklist (Income Cert, Marksheets, Bonafide Student Cert).

### Persona 2: Sunita Devi (Small Landholder Farmer)

- **Age**: 42
- **Gender**: Female
- **Location**: Village in Solapur District, Maharashtra - Rural
- **Category**: General / EWS
- **Occupation**: Farmer (Owner-cultivator)
- **Landholding**: 1.5 Acres (Small farmer)
- **Annual Family Income**: ₹1,10,000 / year

#### Goals
- Secure subsidised drip irrigation equipment and micro-irrigation grants.
- Enroll in crop insurance (PM Fasal Bima Yojana) and seed subsidy programs.

#### Pain Points
- Relying on village middlemen who demand commissions to file online forms.
- Technical jargon in government circulars makes self-assessment difficult.

#### Information Known vs. Unknown
- **Can Provide**: Landholding area (in Acres/Hectares), 7/12 land extract details, Aadhaar number, bank account details.
- **May Not Know**: Specific soil test parameters, scheme category technical names.

#### Typical Questions
- *"Is there a government subsidy for buying a solar pump in Maharashtra?"*
- *"How much financial aid do small farmers get for crop failure?"*

#### Expected System Interaction
Voice or simplified visual inputs → Straightforward eligibility confirmation for agricultural subsidies → Direct link to official state agriculture portal.

### Persona 3: Rajesh Kulkarni (Micro Entrepreneur)

- **Age**: 31
- **Gender**: Male
- **Location**: Suburban Pune, Maharashtra
- **Category**: General
- **Occupation**: Small Manufacturing Business Owner (Food Processing unit)
- **Annual Business Turnover**: ₹18,000,000 / year
- **Employment Status**: Self-employed, employs 6 workers

#### Goals
- Obtain collateral-free business expansion loans under Mudra or PMEGP.
- Apply for MSME technology upgradation subsidies.

#### Pain Points
- High bank interest rates without government credit guarantee backed schemes.
- Unsure whether his unit qualifies under Micro, Small, or Cottage industry brackets.

#### Information Known vs. Unknown
- **Can Provide**: Udyam Registration number, GSTIN, annual turnover, investment in plant & machinery.
- **May Not Know**: Specific subsidy ceilings under PMEGP vs. MUDRA Tarun loans.

#### Typical Questions
- *"What government loan schemes offer interest subvention for food processing MSMEs?"*
- *"Am I eligible for PMEGP if I already have a commercial bank loan?"*

#### Expected System Interaction
Filter by industry sector & investment size → Detailed breakdown of loan subsidies, margin money rules, and participating bank links.

### Persona 5: Ramesh Chandra (Senior Citizen Pensioner)

- **Age**: 67
- **Gender**: Male
- **Location**: Varanasi, Uttar Pradesh - Semi-Urban
- **Category**: General
- **Employment Status**: Retired (Former informal sector worker, no private pension)
- **Annual Family Income**: ₹75,000 / year (Dependant on son)

#### Goals
- Enroll in Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension Scheme (IGNOAPS).
- Obtain Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY) health coverage card for senior citizens.

#### Pain Points
- Mobility issues make frequent trips to government offices difficult.
- Visual impairments and inability to navigate complex web layouts.

#### Information Known vs. Unknown
- **Can Provide**: Age (Aadhaar), BPL status, domicile certificate.
- **May Not Know**: Whether his family's BPL card includes his name as a beneficiary.

#### Typical Questions
- *"Am I entitled to a monthly pension as a 67-year-old in Uttar Pradesh?"*
- *"How can senior citizens get free hospital treatment up to 5 Lakhs?"*

#### Expected System Interaction
High-contrast, large-font layout → Clear checklist of eligibility (Age >= 60, BPL criteria) → Step-by-step guidance on offline/online application channels.

### Persona 6: Anitha Subramanian (Woman SHG Leader)

- **Age**: 35
- **Gender**: Female
- **Location**: Rural Madurai, Tamil Nadu
- **Category**: ST
- **Occupation**: Weaver & Leader of a 12-member Self-Help Group (SHG)
- **Annual Family Income**: ₹95,000 / year

#### Goals
- Secure revolving fund grants and community investment funds for her SHG group.
- Find individual maternity and child welfare schemes for young mothers in her group.

#### Pain Points
- Lack of clarity on group-level eligibility vs. individual member eligibility.
- Difficulty keeping track of multiple state-specific Tamil Nadu schemes (e.g., Pudhumai Penn, Moovalur Ramamirtham Ammal scheme).

#### Information Known vs. Unknown
- **Can Provide**: SHG registration number, group bank account details, member count, individual caste certificates.
- **May Not Know**: Revolving fund credit score requirements mandated by NABARD.

#### Typical Questions
- *"What government grants are available for ST women self-help groups in Tamil Nadu?"*
- *"Which welfare scheme provides financial assistance to pregnant women?"*

#### Expected System Interaction
Dual query mode (Individual vs. Group/SHG eligibility) → Categorized list of women empowerment schemes with clear step-by-step application instructions.

### Persona 7: Vikramjit Singh (Person with Disability - PwD)

- **Age**: 28
- **Gender**: Male
- **Location**: Ludhiana, Punjab - Urban
- **Category**: General
- **Disability Status**: 60% Locomotor Disability (Wheelchair user)
- **Education**: Diploma in Graphic Design
- **Employment Status**: Freelancer / Seeking self-employment grant

#### Goals
- Obtain motorized wheelchair assistance under ADIP (Assistance to Disabled Persons for Purchase/Fitting of Aids and Appliances).
- Apply for PwD self-employment loan subsidies under NHFDC (National Handicapped Finance and Development Corporation).

#### Pain Points
- Digital portals lack screen-reader accessibility and keyboard navigation.
- Unsure whether his UDID (Unique Disability ID) percentage meets specific scheme thresholds (e.g., 40% vs 60%).

#### Information Known vs. Unknown
- **Can Provide**: UDID Card Number, Disability Percentage (60%), Disability Type (Locomotor), Educational Qualification.
- **May Not Know**: Income cap variations across ADIP vs State PwD pensions.

#### Typical Questions
- *"What subsidies can I get for buying a motorized trike with 60% disability?"*
- *"Are there government grants for disabled entrepreneurs starting a digital studio?"*

#### Expected System Interaction
Fully screen-reader accessible interface → Filter schemes specifically by UDID percentage & disability type → Direct download of official application form templates.
